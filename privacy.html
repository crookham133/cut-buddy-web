Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026. Cut Buddy is run by Cut Buddy App LLC. If you live in Washington, Nevada or another state with a consumer health data law, our consumer health data policy also applies to you.

The short version

  • We collect what you tell the app so it can plan and track your food. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never let anyone track you across other apps.
  • Your health answers are yes/no questions. We never store the names of your medications.
  • Your conversations with Buddy are kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • The AI that writes Buddy's replies never receives your name, email, phone number or the names of your medications.
  • You can export everything or delete your account from Settings at any time.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, or your Apple sign-in, and your name.
  • Your profile: age or date of birth, sex, height, weight, body fat if you enter it, your goal and target weight, competition dates, diet, allergies, foods you avoid, cooking situation, budget and time zone.
  • Health answers: yes/no answers to questions such as whether you are pregnant, take blood-sugar medication or have a history of an eating disorder. They decide what advice the app will and won't give you. We ask for your permission before asking them.
  • What you log: meals and foods, water, weigh-ins, your training schedule and events.
  • Conversations with Buddy, and the short notes Buddy keeps about you (for example, "trains at 6 am"). A note about your health is shown to you to keep or delete before Buddy uses it.
  • Team information, if you join a team: your membership; the sharing level you chose, with the version of the wording you agreed to and when; your leaderboard setting; official weigh-ins your coach records from a meet or team scale; notes your coach writes about you; and a log of every time a coach looked at your information.
  • A parent's or guardian's contact details, if you are under 18 and join a team, so we can ask them to approve, and so any later request to share more goes to the same person. We keep it while you are on that team, and you can't see or change it in the app.
  • Usage counts: how many messages you sent and what they cost us to answer, and which days you opened the app and how many times. Never what you wrote or what you did in it.
  • Errors and crashes: when the app hits an error or closes unexpectedly, a description of the error with anything you typed taken out, where in the app's code it happened, which screen you were on, and the app and iPhone software versions.
  • Feedback you send with "Something's off?": your message, and a screenshot if you choose to add one. We remove the location and other details stored inside the picture before we keep it.
  • A referral code, if you enter one from an ambassador: which code, the day you used it, the day your account was made, and whether you later subscribed.
  • Your subscription: whether you have Pro, and when it started, renews or ended, from Apple through RevenueCat. We never see your card details.

Your camera, photos and voice are used on your phone only. Pictures of barcodes and labels, and anything you dictate, are not sent to us. The one exception is a screenshot you choose to attach to feedback.

How we use it

To calculate your targets, plan your meals, answer your questions, show your progress, keep you safe (for example, never planning a weight-loss pace that is unsafe for you), understand how much the service costs to run, fix problems you report and errors the app hits, see how many people use the app and keep using it (as totals, never what anyone wrote), and credit ambassadors for the people they bring. Nothing else.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that run the app for us, under contracts that limit them to doing so: Supabase (hosting), Anthropic (the AI behind Buddy, which receives your messages and the app's context with your name, contact details and medication names removed, and doesn't train on them), Apple (sign-in and purchases), RevenueCat (which manages subscriptions: it receives your purchases from Apple and a random code we make for your account, never your name or email), and our email provider, Google, which delivers the feedback you send to the two founders' inboxes.
  • An ambassador, if you used their referral code: totals only, such as how many people used the code and how many subscribed. Never who you are.
  • Your coach, only if you join a team, at the level you choose. Level 1, which joining means: whether you fueled before and after practice, whether you logged today, whether you are on track for a weigh-in, and the countdown. If you are losing weight faster than a safe rate, your coach is told to check in with you, never with a number. Level 2, only if you turn it on: your weight trend against your plan as a 7-day average, your macros and water against your targets, and meal timing around practice. Level 3, only if you turn it on: what you ate and when, never calories. Assistant coaches see the same, once the head coach allows them. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian approves joining and, separately, levels 2 and 3. If the app limits what it shows you for your health, your coach sees level 1 only. Your coach never sees your health answers, your conversations, Buddy's notes or a daily weight. You can lower your level or leave the team at any time, and it takes effect immediately.
  • Your teammates and coach, on the team leaderboard: days logged in a row, practices fueled, and (if you share level 2) days at your water and protein targets. You are anonymous ("Athlete 37") unless you are 18 or over and choose to show your name, and you can turn it off. Coaches see the top 5 only. Weight, weight lost and calories are never ranked. If the app limits what it shows you for your health, you are never on it.
  • If the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Feedback, and helping improve Buddy

Feedback. When you rate one of Buddy's replies or correct it, we keep the rating with that message and delete it when the message is deleted (after 90 days, or sooner if you delete the conversation). Corrections you choose to keep (for example, a food you don't eat) become notes in "What Buddy knows about me" or changes to your profile, which you can edit or delete.

Help improve Buddy (optional, adults only, off by default). If you turn this on, conversations you rate may be reviewed by named members of our team to improve Buddy's answers, the foods it recognises and the sources it uses. We remove your name, contact details and medication names, store these conversations without your account id, and leave out any conversation about your health. They are de-identified, and a separate code lets us delete them if you turn the setting off or delete your account. We keep them for up to 12 months. We don't use them to train AI models; if we ever want to, we'll ask you separately. People under 18, and accounts set up without numbers, can't turn this on.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website, we keep your email address, your first name, whether you signed up as an athlete, coach, parent or something else, your confirmation that you're 13 or older, and when you joined. We use them only to tell you when Cut Buddy is available. The list is stored with our database provider, and only our server can read it. We don't sell it or share it, and our website has no tracking or advertising tools. To protect the list from automated sign-ups, we keep a scrambled one-way code made from the internet address each sign-up came from, for one day, and then delete it; we can't turn the code back into the address. To leave the list, use the link in any email we send or write to us at support@cutbuddyapp.com. Unsubscribing deletes your sign-up.

How long we keep it

  • Conversations with Buddy: 90 days.
  • Ratings on Buddy's replies: with the message, so 90 days at most.
  • Conversations you shared to help improve Buddy: up to 12 months, or until you turn the setting off.
  • The log of when a coach, or Cut Buddy staff in an emergency, looked at your information: 13 months.
  • A parent's or guardian's contact details: while you are on that team.
  • The website waitlist: until you unsubscribe, or 12 months after you signed up, whichever comes first. The one-way code that protects sign-ups: one day.
  • Feedback you send, and its screenshot: 12 months.
  • Error and crash reports: 90 days.
  • Which days you opened the app: 13 months.
  • A referral: when you delete your account, the code and the days it was used are kept as an anonymous count, with nothing that identifies you, so the ambassador's totals stay right.
  • Everything else: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, everything is deleted from our database straight away, and we check that nothing is left. Encrypted backup copies are deleted within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export everything we hold: Settings → Export my data.
  • Correct your profile at any time in Settings.
  • Delete a conversation, a note, or everything Buddy remembers (Settings → What Buddy knows about me), or your whole account (Settings → Delete my account and data).
  • Withdraw consent to team sharing at any time in Settings → Team (lower your level, turn off the leaderboard, or leave), and to helping improve Buddy in Settings → Buddy.
  • See who looked: Settings → Team → Who's looked lists every time a coach viewed your information, and every time Cut Buddy staff did, and what they viewed.
  • Or write to support@cutbuddyapp.com. We answer within 45 days.

Children and teenagers

Cut Buddy is not for children under 13, and the app won't let anyone under 13 sign up. For users aged 13 to 17, a parent or guardian must approve before any information is shared with a coach, and again before anything beyond level 1 is, and we send each request to them directly. Teenagers are always anonymous on a team leaderboard. Teenagers' data is never used for advertising and never sold. There is a version of this policy for parents and guardians.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each person can reach only their own data, enforced by the database itself. On your phone, your sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain and the app is hidden in the app switcher. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you, and the authorities where required, as the law requires.

Our own access. Nobody at Cut Buddy reads your data by default, founders included. If we must (for example, a safety report about you), one founder can open one person's data for up to an hour, only with a written reason; both founders are emailed before it opens, every read is recorded in a log that can't be edited, and each one appears in your Who's looked list.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
