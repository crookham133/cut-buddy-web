Not medical advice

Cut Buddy gives general nutrition information and planning tools. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it isn't a substitute for a doctor, athletic trainer or registered dietitian. Talk to one before changing how you eat if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, take medication or have a history of an eating disorder. Some features change or switch off for you in those situations, on purpose.

In an emergency, call 911. For crisis support, call or text 988. More help is listed on our support page.

AI answers can be wrong

Buddy uses AI to understand what you type or say and to write its replies. Your targets come from our nutrition calculations, not the AI, but any part of the app can make mistakes: a food entry can be wrong, a meal can be logged incorrectly, or a reply can misunderstand you. Use your judgment, and always check food labels for allergens yourself.
