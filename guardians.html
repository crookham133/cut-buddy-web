Cut Buddy

For parents and guardians

Last updated 24 October 2026. This explains, for the parent or guardian of someone aged 13 to 17, what Cut Buddy collects from them and what it does with it. The full privacy policy applies too.

In short

  • Cut Buddy plans and tracks meals. It never plans dehydration, fasting, sweating down or any other way of making weight, and it won't plan an unsafe rate of weight loss.
  • Your teenager answers a few yes/no health questions first. Those answers make the app more careful: some mean it shows no calorie or weight numbers at all.
  • Nothing is shared with a coach unless your teenager joins a team and you approve by a link we send you directly. Sharing more than the basics needs your approval again. Your teenager never sees or sends these links.
  • We never sell their data, never use it for advertising, and never show it to other users.

What we collect from your teenager

Age, sex, height, weight, goals, diet and allergies, training schedule, the food, water and weigh-ins they log, yes/no health answers, and their conversations with Buddy, the app's assistant. Conversations are deleted after 90 days. We also keep counts of which days they open the app, reports of errors the app hits (never what they typed), any feedback they choose to send us with a screenshot, and a referral code if they used one. Children under 13 can't sign up.

What a coach can see

When you approve joining (level 1): whether they fueled before and after practice, whether they logged today, whether they're on track for a weigh-in, and the countdown. If they're losing weight faster than a safe rate, the coach is told to check in with them, never with a number.

Only if your teenager asks and you approve again: level 2, their weight trend against a safe plan as a 7-day average (never a daily weight), food targets, water and meal timing; level 3, what they eat and when. You can approve less than they ask for.

The coach can also record an official weigh-in from a meet or team scale; your teenager sees it and that the coach entered it. Every time a coach looks, it's listed for your teenager. On the team leaderboard they are always anonymous, and weight, weight lost and calories are never ranked. Never shared: their health answers, their conversations with Buddy, or a daily weight. They can share less, or leave the team, at any time without asking you.

The AI

Buddy's replies are written by an AI model from Anthropic. Before anything is sent to it, we remove your teenager's name, email, phone number and the names of any medications. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. Every number Buddy gives comes from the app's own calculations, not the AI.

Your choices

  • Look at the app together: Settings → What Buddy knows about me shows what Buddy remembers, and Settings → Export my data gives a full copy.
  • Delete the account and everything in it: Settings → Delete my account and data.
  • Withdraw your approval for team sharing, or ask us anything: write to cutbuddyapp@gmail.com. We answer within 45 days.

Cut Buddy gives general nutrition information, not medical advice. If you're worried about your teenager's eating or weight, talk to their doctor, athletic trainer or a registered dietitian. In the US:

  • Crisis or self-harm: the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call or text 988, 24/7.
  • Eating disorder support: the National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline, (866) 662-1235, weekdays 9 AM–7 PM ET.
  • Text support anytime: the Crisis Text Line, text HOME to 741741.
