What it does

  • A plan from your own numbers

    Meals, water and a grocery list built around your training, your schedule and the food you actually eat.

  • Talk to Buddy

    Log a meal in one sentence, or ask what to eat right now. Buddy answers like a friend who knows nutrition.

  • Adapts when life changes

    Practice moves, a competition comes up, you get sick, you travel. Your plan and reminders change with you.

  • Safety built in

    Every number comes from a tested nutrition engine, not the AI, and safety checks run before any advice.

Team features for coaches are optional. Athletes choose what their coach sees, and a parent approves for anyone under 18.

Who it's for

Athletes

Starting with wrestling and combat sports: fueling timed to practices, competitions and travel.

Everyone else

Lifters, busy parents and anyone who wants a plan that fits their week, whether they're cutting, gaining or maintaining.

Cut Buddy gives nutrition information, not medical advice. Medical questions go to your doctor, athletic trainer or a registered dietitian.
