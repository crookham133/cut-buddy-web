For athletes

Planned around your training.

Your day

Hard days get more fuel than rest days. Meals are timed around practice and lifts: a pre-fuel before, recovery after.

A competition on the calendar counts the plan down to it. Water too, in the unit you use.

Log it by saying it.

Talk to Buddy

Tell Buddy what you ate or drank, in your own words. You see exactly what it heard and tap Log it.

Nothing goes in your day until you confirm.

See the trend, not the noise.

Daily weigh-ins bounce. Cut Buddy shows your 7-day average against your plan line, and tells you plainly where you stand: on the line, behind, or going too fast.

Six areas you chose (protein, water, fueling around training, logging and more) scored each week, so you know what to work on.

Safety isn't a setting.

Built in, for everyone

  • Weight loss is capped at a safe weekly rate, and the plan slows itself if you lose faster.
  • It never plans below your certified minimum weight, or below your resting energy.
  • No dehydration, saunas, spitting, laxatives or fasting to make weight, ever.
  • Your health answers change what you're shown. Someone the app shouldn't show numbers to doesn't see them.
  • Every number comes from a tested nutrition engine. The AI writes words, never numbers.
  • Not medical advice: it points you to a doctor, athletic trainer or registered dietitian.
