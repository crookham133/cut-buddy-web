For athletes

Planned around your training.

Your day

Hard days get more food than rest days, and meals are timed around practice and lifts: fuel before, recovery after.

Add a competition and the plan counts down to it. Your water target is planned too, in ounces or milliliters.

Log it by saying it.

Talk to Buddy

Tell Buddy what you ate or drank, in your own words. You see exactly what it heard and tap Log it.

Nothing is logged until you confirm.

See the trend, not the noise.

Daily weigh-ins go up and down. Cut Buddy compares your 7-day average with your plan and tells you where you stand: on track, behind, or losing too fast.

Each week, Cut Buddy scores the six areas you picked, like protein, water and logging, so you know what to work on next.

Safety isn't a setting.

Built in, for everyone

  • Weight loss is capped at a safe weekly rate. If you lose faster than that, the plan slows down on its own.
  • Your plan never goes below what your body needs at rest, or below your certified minimum weight if your sport sets one.
  • It never suggests unsafe ways to make weight.
  • What you tell us about your health shapes what the app shows you. If numbers like calories or weight could do you harm, you won't see them.
  • Every number comes from tested nutrition math, not the AI. Buddy only writes the words.
  • It isn't medical advice. For medical questions, it points you to a doctor, athletic trainer or registered dietitian.
